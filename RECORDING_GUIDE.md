# 🎬 HOW TO RECORD YOUR COMPETITION VIDEO (5 minutes of your time)

## What You Need (All Free, Already on Your Mac)

1. **QuickTime Player** — for screen recording (pre-installed)
2. **Voice Memos** or **QuickTime** — for voice recording (pre-installed)
3. **iMovie** — for editing (pre-installed, or free from App Store)

---

## STEP 1: Record Your Screen (5-8 minutes of raw footage)

1. Open **Google Chrome** and go to `https://pulse-pilot-two.vercel.app/`
2. Make Chrome **full screen** (Cmd + Shift + F or green button)
3. Open **QuickTime Player** → `File` → `New Screen Recording`
4. Click the dropdown arrow next to the record button → select **None** for microphone (we record voice separately)
5. Click **Record** → select your Chrome window
6. **Follow the script in `FINAL_VIDEO_SCRIPT.md`:**
   - Start on Dashboard → scroll slowly
   - Click to Studio → type objective → click Generate Strategy → wait for result → scroll through it
   - Click to Flagsmith Integration → show the live badges and feature flag
   - Click to Demo Store → show Elena (control) → switch to Alex (treatment)
   - Click to Analytics → show stats, highlight the p-value and "underpowered" badge
   - Click to Release Center → show the readiness matrix and approval requirement
   - Click to Activity → show audit entries
   - Go back to Dashboard
7. Press **Cmd + Control + Esc** to stop recording
8. Save as `screen_recording.mov` on your Desktop

**TIPS:**
- Move your mouse SLOWLY and deliberately
- Pause 2-3 seconds on each important element
- Don't worry about being perfect — you can trim in iMovie
- It's OK to record more footage than you need

---

## STEP 2: Record Your Voice (3 minutes)

1. Open **Voice Memos** app (search in Spotlight: Cmd + Space → "Voice Memos")
2. OR use QuickTime: `File` → `New Audio Recording`
3. Find a quiet room
4. Open `FINAL_VIDEO_SCRIPT.md` on your phone or second screen
5. Hit Record and read the script naturally
6. **Don't read like a robot** — pretend you're showing your project to a friend:
   - "Hey, this is PulsePilot AI..."
   - Be enthusiastic about the parts you're proud of
   - Pause naturally between sections
7. Save the recording

**VOICE TIPS:**
- Speak at your natural pace
- If you mess up a line, just pause 2 seconds and say it again — you can cut mistakes in iMovie
- Your natural voice in any accent > any AI voice. Period.

---

## STEP 3: Edit in iMovie (15-20 minutes)

1. Open **iMovie** → Create New → Movie
2. Import your screen recording and voice recording
3. **Timeline layout:**
   ```
   Video track: |===== screen recording (trimmed) =====|
   Audio track: |===== your voice =====|
   Music track: |===== background music (optional, very low volume) =====|
   ```
4. **Add Title Cards** (iMovie → Titles):
   - Opening: "PulsePilot AI"
   - Subtitle: "Autonomous Experimentation Platform"
   - "❌ The Problem" (before Scene 2)
   - "💡 The Solution" (before Scene 3)
   - "🔧 Live Demo" (before Scene 4)
   - "🏆 Why PulsePilot Wins" (before Scene 10)
5. **Trim** the screen recording to match your voice timing
6. **Add transitions** between sections (Cross Dissolve works great, keep them short — 0.5s)
7. **Optional:** Add subtle background music from iMovie's built-in library (keep volume at 10-15%)
8. **Export:** Share → File → Resolution: 1080p 60 → Quality: High → Save

---

## STEP 4: Upload

1. Your exported video should be ~2:45 to 2:55
2. Upload to your competition submission
3. Also upload to YouTube (unlisted) and share the link

---

## QUICK ALTERNATIVE: Record Everything At Once

If you want to skip separate editing:

1. Open QuickTime → New Screen Recording
2. Click dropdown → select your **Built-in Microphone**
3. Now your voice records WITH the screen
4. Navigate the app while reading the script out loud
5. One take, done. Just trim the start/end in QuickTime (Edit → Trim)

This is faster but you can't add title cards or background music. Still perfectly fine for a competition.
